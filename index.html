01 / THE PURPOSE

A football question deserves
a clear explanation.

Passing, progression and regaining possession describe different parts of a performance. Red Half Lab develops reports that make those questions more specific, with the evidence and limits alongside the answer.

The work brings together next-match reports, wider player comparisons and a separate research project. Each has its own purpose.

02RESEARCH IN PROGRESS

Match Pulse.

Form. Cards. Match style.

A separate research project studying single-match and three-match form, red and yellow cards, and patterns of play. The public tracker shares dated aggregate progress.

Historical replays and genuine pre-match records stay separate. Live reliability is not established.
